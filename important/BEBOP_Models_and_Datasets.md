# BEBOP-Gate: Best Hugging Face Models and Datasets (as of 3 Nov 2026)

*Updated 4 Nov 2026 with a no-credits plan (section 6). Findings come from web searches in this session; I did not download or run anything. "Verified" means seen on a model card, paper or project page; "check" means confirm before relying on it.*

## 1. Do we need to train anything?

**No.** BEBOP-Gate uses zero-shot segmentation plus a deterministic verifier. The only "fitting" is **calibrating two thresholds** (k and tau) on a small calibration split of our own scenes. Fine-tuning SAM would add risk and no evidence for the claim. Public datasets below are for **stress-testing and sanity checks**, not training.

## 3. Datasets and footage

| Purpose | Dataset | What it gives | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary (build this)** | **Our own BEBOP-Clearance scenes (~40)** | Ground-truth gaps, occlusions, unanswerable cases built to test abstention | You must capture it; this is the only dataset that tests our exact claim |
| Occlusion stress test | [MOSEv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05630) (5,024 videos; occlusion, low light, weather); [MOSE v1](https://henghuiding.github.io/MOSE) | Shows how segmentation degrades: SAM 2 falls from 76.4% to 50.9% on v2 per the paper | Video object masks, not gap measurements; MOSE v1 is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial; check v2 terms |
| Degraded-visibility check | [ACDC](https://acdc.vision.ee.ethz.ch) (4,006 adverse images: fog, night, rain, snow; 5,509 annotated incl. normal) | Real adverse conditions and an uncertainty-aware segmentation task, matching the NHTSA theme | Driving scenes, not tabletop; check terms |

| Synthetic corruptions | Generated by us with OpenCV (blur, low light, noise, occluding patches) | Free, reproducible, controlled difficulty | Not real-world |

## 4. Footage capture guide (the dataset that matters)

- **Rig:** fixed phone on a tripod, near top-down, 1080p, 30 fps; printed ArUco marker of known size on the same plane.
- **Objects:** one block of known width; two obstacles forming a gap you can adjust.
- **Gap classes:** clear pass (about +30 mm), marginal (about +/-8 mm), clear fail (about -30 mm).
- **Conditions per class:** clean; hand occluding one edge; blur; low light; lookalike distractor; obstacle moved mid-clip.
- **Unanswerable scenes (at least 10):** deciding edge fully hidden, or gap within measurement error. Correct output: UNDECIDED.
- **Ground truth:** calipers or ruler, 3 measurements per gap, stored in `truth.csv` with the camera setup.
- **Split:** about 12 scenes for calibration, about 28 for held-out testing. Do not tune on the test set.
- **Clip length:** 10-20 s each; name files `sNNN_class_condition.mp4`.

## 5. Caveats

Model-card benchmark numbers (especially Muse Glimmer) are self-reported and I could not confirm independent replication. Access to gated SAM weights is not guaranteed. Dataset licenses above are what I saw in this session; confirm before redistributing anything.

| Meta-family local option | [meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B), Apache-2.0, about 17 GB quantised | Keeps a Meta model in the story | Press coverage only; needs a 24 GB GPU |

**Config switch:** set `SEGMENT_BACKEND=meta_api | hf_local | opencv` and `VLM_BACKEND=meta_api | ollama | none`. The verdict code does not change.

**Honesty rule:** if you use substitutes, say so. Label baselines "Qwen3-VL (Ollama)" instead of "Muse Spark", and do not claim SAM 3.1 or Muse Spark if you did not call them. The hackathon scores use of Meta's models, so a local SAM 2.1 / Llama story is weaker than the hosted one; the measured false-approval table matters more in that case.

**Recommended order with no credits:** (1) OpenCV segmentation to get the verifier and benchmark working today; (2) add Grounding DINO + SAM 2.1 as the "real" perception backend if time remains; (3) add an Ollama VLM only for Baseline 1 and explanations.
