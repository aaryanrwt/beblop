THE PROBLEM: PERCEPTION FAILS SILENTLY

1. The Fatal Blind Spot in Embodied AI

Perception-driven AI agents and robotic planners increasingly rely on Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to navigate physical spaces. When an AI model examines a camera feed and suggests an action, how does an autonomous system verify that the action is physically safe?

The Danger: Foundation models do not provide calibrated confidence bounds. When optical conditions degrade — through hand occlusion, lens blur, glare, or sudden obstacle displacement — VLMs guess with high confidence rather than admitting uncertainty.

2. Real-World Motivation: NHTSA Investigation EA26002

In March 2026, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) upgraded its engineering analysis of automated driving systems (EA26002 / PE24031). The core defect identified is the system's failure to recognize its own degraded visibility (fog, low sun glare, dust) and warn the operator before collision.

Asking a generative vision model "Can this object pass through the gap?" produces catastrophic false approvals. The system needs a strict, fail-closed barrier.

3. Empirical Research Evidence (2026 Preprints)

• VAP-TAMP (arXiv 2604.26988)

Demonstrated that in 72 out of 100 real-world robotic trials, unexpected physical changes caused VLM planners to fail. When viewpoint budgets run out, standard algorithms force a binary decision without an abstention option.

• AsgardBench (arXiv 2603.15888)

Benchmarked multimodal planners and reported accuracy drops to 36.1% due to weak spatial grounding and stale state tracking under partial occlusion.

THE SOLUTION: "THE MODEL PROPOSES, THE MEASUREMENT DECIDES"

1. The Tri-State Fail-Closed Verifier

BEBOP-Gate sits between an AI model's plan and physical execution. It enforces an uncompromising rule: An AI model's suggestion is never evidence that an action is safe.

1. Perception Tier

Meta SAM 3.1 & Llama 3.2-Vision propose candidate object outlines across 5 descriptive variants. Untrusted by design; checked for IoU stability (tau = 0.85).

2. Metric Verification

ArUco scale conversion with perspective tilt check. Calculates Euclidean clearance delta: d = gap - (width + margin) with 2-sigma combined uncertainty.

3. Decision Ledger

SHA-256 hash-chained state tracking. If an obstacle drifts >18px or IoU drops <0.8, past approvals are instantly revoked and alternate routes verified.

2. Actionable UNDECIDED Output

When measurement error is within the clearance margin (|d| < 2*sigma_d), BEBOP never guesses. It returns UNDECIDED accompanied by the precise required tolerance:
sigma_target = |d| / k and actionable sensor guidance (e.g. "Re-observe with unoccluded top-down view").
