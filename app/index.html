Decisions Break in a Changing World

Most robotics frameworks assume a static world. In reality, obstacles move, weather degrades optical sensors, and prior decisions become invalid.

THE PROBLEM: FORCED BINARY HALLUCINATIONS

Existing VLM planners (VAP-TAMP, GPT-4V, Gemini) are forced to answer "Yes" or "No". They have no metric grounding, fail silently under fog/occlusion, and retain stale plans when obstacles move.

THE SOLUTION: TRI-STATE VERIFIER + LEDGER
